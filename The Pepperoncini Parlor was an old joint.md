The Pepperoncini Parlor was an old joint. Best Pizza in New Sokyo. At least that's what the owner would have told you 200 years ago. It's lasted at least that long, but the world moves on while some places cannot. The place is on its las legs now, nestled in-between towering mega skyscrapers like an anxious little red brick someone jammed into a cinder block wall when no one was looking. New Sokyo doesn't have a pizza problem, but it was about to have a big horrible nasty tomato sauce mess that would rock The Pepperoncini parlor for the rest of its life.

One late Friday evening there were two people working at the parlor. Roadie was in the back slinging pizzas for the 7 or so people left in the parlor for the night and Allie was at front of house working the counter. Roadie was getting to the end of his shift and needed to talk to his boss, Mr. Pepperoncini, before he left for the weekend. He was hard up for rent that month and needed to pull more hours next week to break even. Roadie went into Mr. Pepperoncini's office to request the hours, but found him sitting in his chair slumped over, his comb-over off and up into the sky, red pooling under the chair.

The police were called immediately. Everyone was detained in the building until the visiting detective could do his job and find out who had done it. Roadie was absolutely trapped at his crappy job until the case had been resolved. Roadie went into the bathroom to wash his face and clean up a bit since he had to get comfortable for a while. The first stall was occupied. That wouldn't be too concerning if a murder hadn't just taken place, but something smelled fishy. Or shitty. A voice emerged from the stall. It was the detective assigned to the case, confined to the can by a terrible twist in his gut. He said that he had made some horrible mistakes that lunch prior. Some way to spend the last day of his career he said, shelling the shitter through some poor bastard's murder case. He knew he wouldn't be going anywhere, which meant neither was Roadie. But, the Detective made an offer: Solve this case for him, just this once, and Roadie would be able to go home. It was an offer Roadie couldn't refuse.

Homes - The Scorned Business Partner

Homes loves pizza, maybe even more than his life. It was his life and the opportunity to open a joint was the best day of his life. He saw The Pepperoncini Parlor through its golden years, when people actually cared for the place and went there regularly. Homes and Pepperoncini were good business partners and got along well enough, but times got hard. People changed, time moved on, and the parlor desperately needed a revamp to stay afloat. This was Homes' plan for the joint. Get a play-place, get a clown, animatronics, something to liven the place up and compete honestly. Homes found out that his partner didn't have the same aspirations for integrity and had instead gotten tangled up with the New Sokyo Mafia to keep the place afloat. Let them clean money through the parlor and the place would never have to close. The Mafia had offered to keep the place standing through any development, any recession, any predatory land grab. When Homes confronted Mr. Pepperoncini about this, Homes got strong-armed out of co-ownership and ended up in a data visualization firm. After becoming sick of slicing pie graphs instead of real pies, Homes sought his revenge on a quiet Friday in New Sokyo.
